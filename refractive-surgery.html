Refractive Surgery

Vision correction starts with the right candidacy assessment.

At Gupta Eye Center, refractive-surgery consultations focus on whether laser vision correction or another refractive option is appropriate for your eyes, prescription and visual goals. Suitability depends on detailed measurements and overall eye health, so the decision begins with careful screening rather than a one-size-fits-all procedure.

Request AppointmentCall Clinic
When to seek evaluation

When an eye assessment may be useful.

  • You are considering reducing dependence on spectacles or contact lenses.
  • Your spectacle/contact-lens prescription has been stable and you want to understand surgical options.
  • You want an assessment of corneal shape, thickness, dry eye and overall eye health before deciding.
Clinical evaluation

What may be checked during assessment.

  • Refraction and visual-acuity testing
  • Corneal topography/tomography and thickness assessment when indicated
  • Tear-film and dry-eye evaluation
  • Pupil size and full eye-health examination
  • Discussion of occupation, expectations, alternatives and risks
Treatment pathways

Treatment is matched to the diagnosis and the eye.

The options below are common approaches that may be discussed. The appropriate plan depends on examination findings, diagnosis, eye health and individual needs.

01

LASIK

A corneal flap is created and laser energy reshapes underlying corneal tissue. Suitability depends on corneal and general eye factors.

02

PRK / surface laser

Laser reshaping is performed on the corneal surface without a LASIK flap; recovery is different and suitability is individualized.

03

Other refractive options

Depending on age, prescription and eye health, non-laser or lens-based alternatives may be considered instead of LASIK.

Your consultation

What to expect at your consultation.

01

History & symptoms

Your vision, symptoms, previous eye care, medicines, health conditions and treatment goals are reviewed first.

02

Eye examination

Tests are selected according to the concern and may include vision testing, eye pressure, slit-lamp examination, dilation or imaging.

03

Plan & follow-up

Findings are reviewed with you, along with appropriate options, expected benefits, limitations, risks and follow-up.
