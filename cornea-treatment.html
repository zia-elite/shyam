Cornea Treatment

Care for the clear front surface of the eye.

Corneal care addresses problems affecting the clear front surface of the eye, including infection, injury, dryness, scarring and disorders of corneal shape. At Gupta Eye Center, treatment is guided by the exact cause, severity and depth of involvement rather than symptoms alone.

When to seek evaluation

When an eye assessment may be useful.

  • Persistent eye pain, redness or light sensitivity
  • Blurred or distorted vision linked to corneal shape or clarity
  • Recurrent abrasion, injury or suspected infection
  • Contact-lens intolerance or significant dry-eye symptoms
  • Progressive corneal thinning or scarring found on examination
Clinical evaluation

What may be checked during assessment.

  • Visual acuity and refraction
  • Slit-lamp examination
  • Fluorescein dye testing for surface defects when indicated
  • Corneal topography/tomography for shape disorders
  • Corneal thickness and tear-film assessment when appropriate
Treatment pathways

Treatment is matched to the diagnosis and the eye.

The options below are common approaches that may be discussed. The appropriate plan depends on examination findings, diagnosis, eye health and individual needs.

01

Medicines and surface care

Lubricants, prescription drops, antimicrobial treatment or other medicines may be used depending on the diagnosis.

02

Optical rehabilitation

Glasses or contact lenses, including specialty lenses in some corneal shape disorders, may improve vision.

03

Procedural or surgical care

Some advanced corneal conditions may need laser treatment, other corneal procedures or partial/full-thickness corneal transplantation.

Your consultation

What to expect at your consultation.

01

History & symptoms

Your vision, symptoms, previous eye care, medicines, health conditions and treatment goals are reviewed first.

02

Eye examination

Tests are selected according to the concern and may include vision testing, eye pressure, slit-lamp examination, dilation or imaging.

03

Plan & follow-up

Findings are reviewed with you, along with appropriate options, expected benefits, limitations, risks and follow-up.
