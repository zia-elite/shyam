Cataract Treatment

Clear information about cloudy lenses, surgery and lens replacement.

Cataract assessment at Gupta Eye Center focuses on how lens clouding is affecting everyday vision — including blur, glare, night driving, colour perception and changing spectacle power. When surgery is appropriate, the consultation covers examination findings, eye measurements, lens options and the expected follow-up pathway.

When to seek evaluation

When an eye assessment may be useful.

  • Cloudy, hazy or blurred vision that is gradually worsening
  • Glare or halos around lights, especially at night
  • Colours appearing less bright or increased sensitivity to light
  • Frequent changes in spectacle power or difficulty with reading/driving
Clinical evaluation

What may be checked during assessment.

  • Visual acuity and refraction
  • Slit-lamp examination of the lens and front of the eye
  • Dilated retinal examination when appropriate
  • Eye measurements used to calculate an intraocular lens (IOL)
  • Review of other eye conditions that may affect visual outcome
Treatment pathways

Treatment is matched to the diagnosis and the eye.

The options below are common approaches that may be discussed. The appropriate plan depends on examination findings, diagnosis, eye health and individual needs.

01

Observation and updated glasses

Early cataract symptoms may sometimes be managed with brighter lighting and an updated spectacle prescription until surgery is needed.

02

Cataract surgery

The cloudy natural lens is removed and replaced with an artificial intraocular lens. The exact technique and lens choice are individualized.

03

Post-operative follow-up

Eye drops and follow-up visits help monitor healing, eye pressure, inflammation and vision after surgery.

Your consultation

What to expect at your consultation.

01

History & symptoms

Your vision, symptoms, previous eye care, medicines, health conditions and treatment goals are reviewed first.

02

Eye examination

Tests are selected according to the concern and may include vision testing, eye pressure, slit-lamp examination, dilation or imaging.

03

Plan & follow-up

Findings are reviewed with you, along with appropriate options, expected benefits, limitations, risks and follow-up.
