Retina Treatment

Retinal symptoms deserve timely assessment—some are urgent.

Retinal symptoms are assessed with particular attention to changes that may need prompt care — such as sudden flashes, a new shower of floaters, a curtain-like shadow or sudden loss of vision. Evaluation may include a dilated retinal examination and imaging such as OCT before the next step is discussed.

When to seek evaluation

When an eye assessment may be useful.

  • New or suddenly increasing floaters
  • Flashes of light in one or both eyes
  • A dark curtain or shadow moving across part of the vision
  • Sudden distortion, central blur or loss of vision
  • Vision changes in a person with diabetes or known retinal disease
Clinical evaluation

What may be checked during assessment.

  • Dilated retinal examination
  • Optical coherence tomography (OCT) when indicated
  • Retinal photography or other imaging
  • Ultrasound if the retina cannot be seen clearly or more information is needed
  • Review of diabetes, blood pressure, trauma, high myopia and previous eye procedures
Treatment pathways

Treatment is matched to the diagnosis and the eye.

The options below are common approaches that may be discussed. The appropriate plan depends on examination findings, diagnosis, eye health and individual needs.

01

Laser or cryotherapy

Small retinal tears or certain retinal conditions may be treated with laser or freezing treatment, depending on diagnosis.

02

Intravitreal injections

Some retinal diseases are treated with medicines injected into the eye to control abnormal blood vessels, leakage or inflammation.

03

Retinal surgery

A detached retina or other complex retinal problem may require surgery. The method depends on the type and extent of disease.

Your consultation

What to expect at your consultation.

01

History & symptoms

Your vision, symptoms, previous eye care, medicines, health conditions and treatment goals are reviewed first.

02

Eye examination

Tests are selected according to the concern and may include vision testing, eye pressure, slit-lamp examination, dilation or imaging.

03

Plan & follow-up

Findings are reviewed with you, along with appropriate options, expected benefits, limitations, risks and follow-up.
