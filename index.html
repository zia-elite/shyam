About Dr. Shyam Gupta

A medical career shaped by one clear goal: make eye care easier to understand and access.

My medical journey began in Lucknow at King George's Medical University, where I studied from 1998 to 2004. It later expanded through healthcare work with Medical Indian 13 (MI13) and clinical experience associated with Dr. RML Hospital / PGIMER, New Delhi. Today, I have brought that experience back to Lucknow through Gupta Eye Center.

One idea has stayed consistent throughout that journey: healthcare should be easier for people to understand and navigate. At Gupta Eye Center, that translates into a practical style of ophthalmic care — listen carefully, examine thoroughly, explain findings in clear language, and make treatment decisions with the patient rather than around the patient.

An ophthalmology consultation may cover blurred vision, changing spectacle power, cataract symptoms, glaucoma evaluation, retinal concerns, corneal problems, eye irritation, childhood vision concerns and other eye-health symptoms. The exact evaluation depends on your history and examination.

No. Cataract surgery is generally considered when the cloudy lens is affecting useful vision or daily activities. The timing is individualized after examination and discussion of benefits, risks and alternatives.

Existing optic-nerve damage from glaucoma usually cannot be reversed. Treatment is aimed at lowering eye pressure and reducing the risk of further damage, using medicines, laser or surgery as appropriate.

A sudden increase in floaters, flashes of light, a dark curtain or shadow, or sudden loss of vision can be warning signs of retinal detachment or another urgent eye problem. Seek prompt eye or emergency care.

No. Refractive surgery requires careful screening. Prescription stability, corneal thickness and shape, dry eye, pupil size, general eye health, medications and other health factors may affect suitability.

Children may not recognize or report blurred vision. Screening can help identify refractive error, amblyopia (lazy eye), strabismus and other eye problems while visual development is still taking place.
